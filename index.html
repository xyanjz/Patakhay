What goes into your show

The fireworks we use most, and what each one does in the sky.

Sky cake

A box of tubes that fire one after another. Each shot climbs and bursts, building to a big finish.

Made by us

Shot stick

Held upright, it fires 7 shots into the sky one by one. Like a small cake in your hand.

Made by us

Anar

A ground fountain that sprays a tall shower of silver sparks, then fades softly.

Made by us

Shurli

A rocket that shoots up with a bright trail and ends in one big blast.

Made by us

Aerial shells

Launched high into the sky to open into wide bursts and gold willow.

Safety

        
01Trained crew handling
Only our trained crew set up and light the show. Guests never handle sky items. Sparkler sticks go only to adults, after a short safety brief.

        
02Legal clearances
We manufacture under license and check your venue's rules and local permission requirements before the date is confirmed.

        
03Safety distance
We mark a safety radius around the launch area based on the products used. Guests, cars and marquees stay outside it. Water, sand and an extinguisher are always on site.

        
04Weather plan
We keep material covered and watch the wind and forecast. If it is not safe to fire, we agree a new time with you instead of going ahead.

      

      

        
Questions

        
How does pricing work?
Packages show a starting price where available. Your final quotation comes after we check the date, venue, guest count and the effects you want.

        
How early should I book?
For weddings, 2 to 4 weeks ahead, especially from November to February. Birthdays can often be arranged within a few days.

        
Can you do fireworks in a marriage hall?
Indoors we use cold pyro only: smokeless, low-heat fountains. Sky items like cakes, sticks and shurli need open sky.

        
Which cities do you cover?
We serve across Punjab, including Lahore, Faisalabad, Gujranwala, Sialkot, Rawalpindi, Multan, Sargodha, Sheikhupura, Gujrat, Sahiwal and Bahawalpur. Tell us your city and we confirm travel with you.

        
How do I confirm my booking?
Once you are happy with the plan and quotation, a small advance locks your date. The rest is paid on the event day.

        
Do I need permission for fireworks?
Some venues and areas have their own rules. We check your venue's policy and local requirements with you before the date is confirmed.

        
Can I choose when the fireworks go off?
Yes. Tell us the moments that matter, like the baraat arrival, cake cutting or rukhsati, and our crew fires on your cue.
